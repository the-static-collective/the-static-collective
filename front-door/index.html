So, what's Abundent?

We're testing a simple idea: people have more to offer each other than they know how to share.

Sometimes what you need and what somebody else can offer are a few steps apart. We're building tools to make those steps easier to find.

We start with you. Hopefully, we don't stop there.

Still a little messy.

The workshop has plenty of projects, and we're still learning how to present them. Some things work; some are sketches; some are deliberately unfinished.
