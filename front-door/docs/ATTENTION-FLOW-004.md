# ATTENTION-FLOW-004 — Proximity by interest, not by identity

**Status:** working design and a small local-only Porch implementation, on a draft branch. This is not a deployed matching, social-network, or civic-participation service.

## The particular

People arrive with an immediate felt direction:

- **Something coming in:** I could use help, information, supplies, time, attention.
- **Something going out:** I have surplus, a skill, a tool, a few minutes, an idea.
- **Something to understand:** I have noticed a question, contradiction, opening, or problem.

These are **moments, not types of people**. The same person can move in every direction over a single day. Do not classify people as needy, useful, generous, productive, or unproductive. Nobody owes their history, moral identity, or a profile to participate.

The other axis is **attention available now**, not a measure of the person's worth: half a minute, a few minutes, or a little more room. People must be able to do one tiny thing and leave, without a timer, streak, funnel, guilt, or required account.

## The human promise

> Bring whatever is on your mind. Leave with something that helps.

The visitor does not have to know that their small actions might contribute to broader community capacity or the accumulation of useful culture. They **do** need transparent notice of what their particular click actually does, what is stored or shared, what is speculative, and where someone else has decision authority. Simplicity is not permission to conceal consequences.

No one needs to enroll in civilization-building. Ordinary voluntary and useful acts build culture in the open.

## Shape: interest proximity

```text
a present burden / surplus / curiosity
        +
a few seconds or minutes of attention
        ↓
one small voluntary action or useful artifact (works alone)
        ↓
optional nearby possibilities relevant to the *particular*
        ↓
separate, explicit human consent and receiver permission
        ↓
actual accepted action / report / independent confirmation
        ↓
portable helpful method, if someone chooses to share it
        ↓
other people may discover it when their own moment makes it relevant
```

**Proximity is not a friend graph.** A person asking about spare bread may be near a food-sharing method; a person holding tools may be near a repair request; a student with ten minutes may be near one answerable question. The *interest* can form a temporary connection without building dossiers of people.

### Relevance must remain inspectable

If an optional suggestion service becomes real later, every candidate should identify:

1. What particular interest or explicit search made it relevant.
2. What real source, owner, freshness and location constraints apply.
3. Whether it is only a method, a proposed invitation, or actually available.
4. What little step would advance it, and how much attention that step demands.
5. What consent, safety and receiving authority remain open.
6. A meaningful exit, refusal, ignore and correction path.

Do not infer private location, health, family circumstance or relationship from open-ended text. Never rank human beings by generosity, need, attention span, willingness or compliance. Do not maximize session duration. No auto-publishing a person's request, broadcasting a match, or contacting a stranger solely from semantic similarity.

## Interface law: fulfill the current moment

- **A glance:** produce one usable sentence or next question, no request to open further doors.
- **A few minutes:** produce a bounded first action, a progress condition, and a privacy/consent boundary.
- **More room:** produce a small preparation step, still without assuming consent to outreach.
- **A refusal or an unanswered offer:** count as a valid and useful result when it resolves the next choice.
- **No network or other participant:** the small local artifact still helps.

The Porch implementation of this slice adds the local attention selector to NEXT-MOVE-003. It is **not serialized into the visitor's note** or submitted to the current private queue. No extra analytics, storage, AI call, profile or backend path. The current deterministic suggestions do not match people or measure outcomes.

## Interoperable but not indistinguishable

- Human Porch owns the immediate, legible need/offer/question experience.
- JAR;VIS may *propose* relevant doors or bounded actions and receives no implied authority to execute.
- reLATTE may preserve crossing/receipt semantics, never replace individual receiver decisions.
- BananaSpork and Garden may handle specific accepted community work under their own authorization rules.
- webZ and GRAFT may help expose independent worlds and artifacts, not force them into one canonical directory.
- Creative machines can optionally celebrate/share a consented outcome; a generated story must not masquerade as completed field work.

These are candidate future connections, **not** proof of live cross-system interoperability.

## Success

Measure whether the visitor left with something actually useful even alone; later whether a real recipient explicitly accepted a bounded action; then whether a confirmed act created a reusable method, with permission.

Avoid vanity measures: time on site, scroll depth, AI tokens, registrations, raw matches, sessions, or the number of repositories invoked.

**The background structure can be sophisticated. The foreground action must stay small, voluntary, and true.**
